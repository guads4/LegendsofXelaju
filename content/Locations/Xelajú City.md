# TL;DR

- Capital of the kingdom of Xelajú
- Largest most developed city
  - Birthplace of the current Industrial Revolution
- [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Ixzamna]] is the patron god of the city
- Consists of an upper and lower city

Originally known just as Xela, this was a major human village. Yokte's came to this city first to begin tra ng. After subtly (and patiently) teaching the humans how to properly interact with under-dwellers and elves, the city became a diverse hub. Many under-dwellers and elves began to settle down in this city bringing with them knowledge and technology and some written language from their longer lives. Tribes from all around the region would come for trade and education. When the thirteen tribes unified, the city, now the capital, was renamed Xelajú City. In the past century technology has advanced rapidly. Roads were made connecting all of the kingdoms, a standardized written language was taught to everyone in the kingdom. In Xela, a major focus was educating the population so they worked to mass produce texts. In efforts to more efficiently do this, [[TortusImage.png|TortusImage]] invented a printing press (and the artificer class). This kicked off a proper Industrial Revolution.

In modern day, the population of Xela has boomed. Two schools were formed, one for general education and magic [[magicschool]], the other was an [[artificer school and research center]] founded by [[TortusImage.png|TortusImage]]. The old ~~mercenary~~ adventurers guild was reformed and standardized in all the cities. Here in Xelajú [[TortusImage.png|TortusImage]] sponsored these improvements and gives licensed adventurers discounts on their supplies.
