### TL;DR

- Capital of the kingdom of Xelajú
- Largest, most developed city
  - Birthplace of the current Industrial Revolution
- [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Ixzamna]] is the patron god of the city
- Consists of an upper and lower city

# How it started and where we are now

Originally known just as Xela, this was a major human village. Yokte's came to this city first to begin trading. After years of subtly (and patiently) teaching the humans how to properly interact with under-dwellers and elves, the city became a diverse hub. Many under-dwellers and elves began to settle down in this city bringing with them knowledge, technology and different languages from their longer lives. Tribes from all around the region would come for trade and education. When the thirteen tribes unified, the city, now the capital, was renamed Xelajú City. In the past century, technology has advanced rapidly. Roads were made connecting all of the kingdoms, [[Ah Kʼin|the priest]] was blessed by [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Ixzamna]] with a standardized written language to teach to everyone in the kingdom. In Xela, a major focus was educating the population so they worked to mass produce texts. In efforts to more efficiently do this, [[Tortus|Tortus]] invented a printing press (and the artificer class). This kicked off a proper Industrial Revolution.

In modern day, the population of Xela has boomed. Two schools were formed, Xelajú University for general education and magic, the other was a research center and artificer school called Tortus Magical Network of Technologists (TMNT). The old ~~mercenary~~ adventurers guild was reformed and standardized in all the cities. Here in Xelajú [[Tortus|Tortus]] sponsored these improvements and gives licensed adventurers discounts on their supplies.

## The Upper City:

The upper city is sat upon a "man" made platform. This was the first cooperative effort made by all children of [[Pantheon|the gods]]. This is where the efforts to connect the united tribes via roads came from. The upper city is immaculate with stone building piercing the sky. There is often music playing in the streets, and markets along every corner. Beautiful parks are scattered throughout. A Pok Ta Pok field open for folks to play at any time.

Many of the upper echelon live here in magically powered houses. [[Ajavinel Chichu' Yohl|The queen's]] temple is here, the stories of the gods etched and painted onto the walls. The newest addition to the base of the temple depicts the ingenuity of those yoktes who saved the lives of countless under-dwellers and the union of the three siblings to create this beautiful place.

## The Lower City:

Using the mathematical wisdom from [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Ixzamna]] the upper city was built to collect water in the lower city. This initial reservoir is then filtered and used for farming as well as household water. The lower city's architecture focuses on farming and irrigation. Lush fields and clusters of houses made of bamboo and clay surround the upper city on all sides. Nearer to the ramps leading to the upper city, where there is more shade, is where you'll find markets and vendors. A majority of the adults and elders living here have jobs related to farming, but many adolescents travel daily to the upper city for different specializations!
