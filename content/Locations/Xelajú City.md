- Capital of the kingdom of Xelajú
- Largest most developed city
  - has stone streets
    home of the current  IndustrialRevolution
- [Ixzamna](Xelajú/Player%20Access/Pantheon.md#Ixzamna%20-%20God%20of%20Wisdom%20and%20life) is the patron god of this city

Originally known just as Xela, this was a major human village. Yokte's came to this city first to begin trading. After subtly (and patiently) teaching the humans how to properly interact with  under-dwellers and  elves, the city became a diverse hub. Many  under-dwellers and  elves began to settle down in this city bringing with them knowledge and technology and some written language from their longer lives. Tribes from all around the region would come for trade and education. When the thirteen tribes unified, the city, now the capital, was renamed Xelajú City. In the past century technology has advanced rapidly. Roads were made connecting all of the kingdoms, a standardized written language was taught to everyone in the kingdom. In Xela, a major focus was educating the population so they worked to mass produce texts. In efforts to more efficiently do this, [Tortus](Xelajú/NPCs%20\(Xela\)/Tortus.md) invented a printing press (and the artificer class). This kicked off a proper  IndustrialRevolution.

In modern day, the population of Xela has boomed. Two schools were formed, one for general education and magic [[magicschool]], the other was an [[artificer school and research center]] founded by [Tortus](Xelajú/NPCs%20\(Xela\)/Tortus.md). The old ~~mercenary~~ adventurers guild was reformed and standardized in all the cities. Here in Xelajú [Tortus](Xelajú/NPCs%20\(Xela\)/Tortus.md) sponsored these improvements and gives licensed adventurers discounts on their supplies.
