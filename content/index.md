# Reading Conventions:

Mayan languages use five vowels. Using the Spanish convention, these vowels are pronounced as follows:
_a_ is like the _a_ in “far” or “father.”\
_e_ is like the _e_ in “obey” or “prey.”\
_i_ is like the double _e_ in “see” or “bee.”\
_o_ is like the _o_ in “hello” or “open.”\
_u_ is like the double _o_ in “zoo” or “boo.”\
_x_ is pronounced as _sh_. eg Xibalba = She-ball-bah

# Telenovela Tropes:

I am hoping to give this a telenovela/soap opera vibe so please feel free to sprinkle any common tropes into your characters! Some examples below:

- Secret siblings 
  - someone from the poor family is actually related to the rich family
- Fake death / surprise return 
  - presumed dead character comes back with a grudge
- Affairs with consequences 
  - messy love triangles, mistaken paternity, secret babies
- Blackmail 
  - someone knows too much and is milking it
- Talk show scandal 
  - a secret gets revealed on air, blowing everything up
- Inheritances and hidden wills 
  - money shifting hands, but only under certain conditions
- A character has a double life 
  - mild-mannered sibling is actually a criminal mastermind
- Medical drama 
  - coma reveals, rare disease scares, swapped hospital records
