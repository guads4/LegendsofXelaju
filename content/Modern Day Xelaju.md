> [!important] This is a story about love
>
> - Though, it's not _necessarily_ a "love story". Just a story that understands that the one and only motivator is love. It's why we get up in the morning. Why we work jobs we hate. Why we continually strive to be better.
> - How does love affect your decisions? Who or what do you love above all else? A person? A god? A thing? Would you die for love? Kill for love? Live for love? What will you do when your love is challenged?

In the world of Cab, the kingdom of Xelajú is going through an industrial revolution. Years ago, the god [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Itzamna]] granted knowledge to a tortle named [[Tortus]] to imbue objects with magic. Tortus became the first artificer, and along with his homunculus service dog, they have paved the way for a new era! Magic is now available to those who to those who were not blessed with those abilities! Radios are found in every home, self propelled carriages are becoming more and more common. These advancements have enticed folks of all backgrounds to settle down in [[Xelajú City]]. People of any species come together to play the ball game Pok Ta Pok. Now, nearly every city in the kingdom has a Pok Ta Pok field and an annual national tournament happens before the harvest!

Many in upper [[Xelajú City]] have seen their businesses boom as people are flocking to the new [[Ajavinel Chichu' Yohl]] funded Xelajú University as well as the Tortus Magical Network of Technologists (TMNT). However, this sudden influx in population has strained lower [[Xelajú City]]'s ability to keep up with agricultural demands. [[Ajavinel Chichu' Yohl|Ajavinel Yohl]] and [[Tortus]] have made improving agricultural technology a top priority. In the meantime, [[Ajavinel Chichu' Yohl|the queen]] is working with the other nobles in the kingdom to bring in extra resources for the town. With the promise of brighter days comes the Festival of Maltyox (Festival of Gratitude)! One of the biggest holidays in the land centered on celebrating community!
