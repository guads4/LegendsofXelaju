The gods live in the upperworld and the underworld, and can travel through Cab (Earth/middleworld) as they please. Often, appearing as humanoid, full animal, or some sort of hybrid. Thirteen creator gods and thirteen jaguar gods keep the three planes in balance, ruling over many other minor gods (not all listed below). The gods can appear in any gender, though some may prefer one over another. The list below uses the word "god" as gender neutral, with pronouns stated where relevant.

# Creator Gods:

### Ixzamna - God of Wisdom and Life

- Ruler of all other gods
- Patron of astrology and medicine

### Tz'aqol - The "Framer"

- "Framing" refers to making something by putting things together (i.e., a building from stone or adobe, a meal from various ingredients, or a woven cloth from individual threads)

### B'itol - The "Shaper"

- "Shaper" refers to one who makes something by modeling (i.e., pottery from clay, or a sculpture from carved stone), thus giving shape to an otherwise amorphous substance

### Ku'Ku'Lkan - God of the Sky

- Tends to appear as a snake with the feathers of a quetzal

### Cacoch - God of Communication (He/Him)

- Considered "Grandfather" god
- Created the water lily from which the creator and jaguar gods sprang from
  - The water lily is a symbol of the ruler of Xibalba
  - Priests drink lily water to facilitate communication with the dead

### Chimalat - Goddess of Giants (She/Her)

### Ixumcane and Ixpiyacoc - Goddesses of Love and Relationships (Both She/Her)

- Grandparents to [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]]
- The original "Daykeepers"
  - Able to see with divine foresight.

> [!quote] "May it dawn so that we are called upon and supported, so that we are remembered by framed and shaped people"
> Said when they were creating humans

### Huracan - God of Storms and Creativity

- Considered the Heart of the Sky
- Helped to think of what to make for each round of creation
- Eldest brother to Räxa and Ch'i'p
  - The three act as a triad to control the wind and skies

### Räxa - God of Lightning

- Name means sudden thunderbolt

### Ch'i'p - God of Thunder

- Name means youngest thunderbolt

### Hun-Ixim - God of Maize

### Nohocacyum - God of Justice

- Sibling to the death god Kisin
- Enemy to all who wish ill to those they have helped to create

# Jaguar Gods (Gods of Xibalba):

### Hun-Came - Lord of Xibalba

- Hun means "one", came means "death"
- Oversees the [[Xibalba|The Underworld]]
- Once defeated by [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]]
  - Has since reincarnated; very bitter about it

### Vucub-Came - God of Suffering

- Also defeated by [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]] and bitter about it

### Cuchumaquic - God of Disease

- People pray to Cuchumaquic and Vucub-Came to help ease their suffering and disease

### Baalham - Protector of people and communities

- Often works with Nohocacyum

### Bolon Yokte - God of Transitions and Natural Disasters

- often has altars at caves and bodies of water
  - considered liminal/transitional places between the middleworld and the underworld

### Chac Xib Chac - God of the Sea

- Part of a triad with K'awiil and Kinich Ahau
- Often depicted with a shell ear

### K'awiil - Goddess of Infants and Babies

- Part of a triad with K'awiil and Kinich Ahau
- Often depicted either as lightning or as a child with lightning abilities
- Very mischievous

### Kinich Ahau - God of Fire (He/Him)

- Part of a triad with K'awiil and Chac Xib Chac
- Ex-husband to Ixchel

### Ixchel - Godess of Midwifery, Medicine and the Whole Moon (She/Her)

- Ex-wife to Kinich Ahau

### Ixquic - Goddess of the Waning Moon (She/Her)

- Mother of [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]]
- The waning moon is the second moon in the sky of Cab
  - It is called the waning moon because part of it is missing causing it to permanently look like a waning crescent moon

### Zipacna - God of the Earth

### Tunkuruchu - God of Omens

- Appears as an owl to foretell death
- Appears as a quetzal to foretell life

### Kisin - God of Death

# Minor Gods:

### Acan - God of Intoxication and Wine

### Acat - God of Tattoos

### Ah-Muzen-Cab - God of Bees and Honey

### The Bacabs - The Sky Carrying Gods

- Four gods that each represent one of the cardinal directions
  - Each direction is associated with a color white with north, red with east, yellow with south, and black with west.
- Often present at crossroads

### The Bolontiku - The Gods of Regeneration

- Council of nine underworld gods that decide where the souls of the deceased will be reincarnated

### Buluc Chabtan - God of War, Violence, Sacrifice, and Gambling

### Cabrakan - God of Mountains and Earthquakes

### Camazotz - God of the Night and Bats

### Chac Uayab Xoc - God of Rain and Patron Deity of Fishermen

### Ek Chuah - God of Cacao and Commerce

### Hun-Batz - Patron of the Arts

- One of the two step-brothers of [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]]
- Howler Monkey God

### Hun-Chouen - Patron of the Arts

- One of the two step-brothers of [[Myths, Legends and Old Traditions#The Legend of the Hero Twins|the Hero Twins]].
- Howler Monkey God

### K'inich Ahau - God of the Sun

### Maximon - God of Travelers, Merchants, Medicine, Mischief and Fertility (They/Them)

### Nakon - God of War

- Said to be stronger than all the other gods of war in every other religion

### Wuk Sip - God of the Hunt

### Yum Kaax - God of the Woods and Wild Nature
