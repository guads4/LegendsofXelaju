Before mortals existed as we know it there were only [[Pantheon|the gods]]. After millennia of living in the celestial plane, [[Pantheon|the gods]], in their benevolence, saw fit to use their immense power to create more life. Thirteen [[Pantheon#Creator Gods|creator gods]] began with the creation of Cab followed by three rounds of creating intelligent life.

They began by making what we now know as the middleworld, or Cab. They made the sun and the moons. The earth and the seas. All existing in perfect balance. They then created animals to take care of Cab. However, they still wished for more. They wished for intelligent life to enjoy all that they have created.

![[Images/deep-gnomeXela.webp|363]]They looked at what they had made, and saw earth. They first made the under-dwellers; Gnomes, dwarves and the like. The under-dwellers were hard working miners. They trusted in themselves and each other for success. They were a tight knit group, always willing to help another tribe. They made "quick" work (multiple generations and counting) at creating a life in their tunnels underground. However, because they had each other and spent most of their lives developing tunnels, they had no need or time to worship the gods. ^under-dwellers

> [!quote] "Cab provides" or "May Cab Provide"
> Popular under-dwellers saying; often said when saying goodbye or hello

![[Images/khoravarXela.webp|318x208]][[Pantheon|The gods]] wished to make life that was more free. Free to roam and learn about the world that the gods created. So the elves were made. The elves tend to live nomadic lives learning what they can about the land they pass through. Most are curious by nature and will study whatever they find, be it rocks, trees, animals, rivers etc. to understand how and why it works. Some elves went to live with the under-dwellers to learn how they lived and to do trade. They also helped to develop new mining techniques. These elves became well respected amongst the under-dwellers. When a terrible earthquake threatened to destroy much of what the under-dwellers built, these elves quickly came up with plans to prevent as much loss of life as they could. After much tragedy was avoided, these under-dwelling elves were named Yoktes, after [[Pantheon#Bolon Yokte - God of Transitions and Natural Disasters|Bolon Yokte]] (these are the drows of this world). Even now, they continue to develop contingencies for natural disasters. Even with all this progress made, [[Pantheon|the gods]] wanted _**more**_. ^elves

![[Images/humanXela.webp|249]]Still wanting more, [[Pantheon#Hun-Ixim (hoon iks-eem) - God of Maize|Hun-Ixim]] offered up his maize to his fellow creator gods. And so Humans were made from maize. Humans had the tenacity of the under-dwellers and the curiosity of the elves. Their shorter lifespan (compared to the centuries of under-dwellers and, in rare cases, up to thousands of years for elves) led to a greater need and respect of [[Pantheon|the gods]]. During their worship, [[Pantheon#Ixzamna (iks-ahm-nah) - God of Wisdom and Life|Ixzamna]] bestowed upon one early [[Ah Kʼin|Priest]] the divine knowledge of the stars, the calendar, and the written word. While [[Pantheon|the gods]] would never vocalize this, most believed that humans were their favorites. As a result, early humans tended to have certain prejudices against the under-dwellers and elves. Under-dwellers were assumed to be unintelligent laborers, and elves were seen as snooty and socially inept with one track minds. However, humans were not perfect. They had shorter tempers, were less cooperative, and quicker to verbal and physical fights than their predecessors. At first, humans were given a wide berth to develop on their own. Eventually, some brave Yoktes began to try to trade with them. The kingdom of Xelajú was created around an ever growing diverse market which was known locally as [[Xelajú City|Xela]]. This would later become the capital, [[Xelajú City]]. Humans living in modern day [[Xelajú City|Xela]] don't harbor strong prejudices against their elder "siblings", although some still lingers. The current [[Ajavinel Chichu' Yohl|Ajavinel]] (queen) is working to breakdown these prejudices and has a variety of species in her court. ^humans

Animal like species, such as aarakocra or dragonborn, are distant descendants of the gods. Early humans revered these people due to their blood relation to [[Pantheon|the gods]], however, modern day humans treat them as equals. Species from other planes are not common, but not unheard of. Although many, even now, are wary of those from other planes as the creator gods did not have a hand in their creation.
