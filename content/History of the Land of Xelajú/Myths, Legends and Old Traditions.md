## Mystery of the immortal elf

- for humans and under-wellers, the story goes that some elves can live thousands of years
  - its very rare, and its thought that maybe only a handful of non elves have ever met any
  - think they are probably hermits, perhaps hiding from others or taking on alter egos throughout the ages
- the elven myth says that its just one elf thats ever been known to live past a thousand years older
  - no one has seen them in centuries nor even remembers their name
  - most believe they arent real and that it came up as a way to mess with humans
  - others believe they learned the secret to immortality and climbed up the Ceiba Tree to the celestial plane

## Coming of Ages

- concept of ages not by specific numbers but by stage of life.
  - Infant, child, adolescent, adult, elder, ancestor
  - traditionally, transitions from one stage to another often accompanied by set of trials/challenges to ensure the person has learned the lessons culturally accepted of their "stage" and are ready to move on.
    - Commemorated with party or festival (when celebrating many people at once)
    - some trials emphasize teamwork and others are meant to show what you can do on your own
    - "failing" to pass is not seen as a moral failure but as a way for the gods to show you what aspects you need to work on
  - In modern times a majority of people have moved away from the trials
    - mainly just celebrate when they reach the new adult phase
      - generally people get this phase when they start a career and/or a family
      - beginning to look more like we understand it today
        - many people (generally poorer) are considering "new adult" as ~18-20 as they enter the work force sooner than rich people
          - rich people can go to school or travel
            - school is not ridiculously expensive, but you can either go to school or work
              - going to school part time is frowned upon
